Hello viewers! My name is Aniya Sparrow and I am a senior at the University of Missouri. I am currently working to get my bachelor’s in Journalism with an emphasis in Strategic Communication, as well as a minor in Business. The goal is to one day work in the advertising field as an Account Manager. I want to be able to lead teams in creating brand campaigns that will promote products and further expand the tone of a brand or company.

We all know that finding our own sense of style can include high end pieces of fashion, ones that you see in the best designer stores all across the world. However, it’s pretty obvious that fashion can be expensive. At The Luxury Mindset, the intention is to create a place where people can find fashion pieces and where to get them at a rate they can afford. I, myself, have always struggled to find a style with clothing exactly in my price range. This website is a tool to help those who struggle with the same problem.

I want to be able to make people feel confident in their clothing. My goal is that everyone can feel confident enough in themselves. To do that, I find solutions that will ultimately make it easier for everyone to be passionate about what they wear.
