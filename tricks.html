The biggest mistake that people make when it comes to purchasing high-end pieces at a discounted rate, is the fact that there are fake clothes or products that get sold cheap. It makes it easy to make a quick profit if people think they can convince others they have authentic items. Here are some tips and tricks to identifying what is real designer versus fake designer.

How To Identify Real LOUIS VUITTON

  • LOUIS VUITTON may also have PARIS on the end of it's brand name. Those letters should be in gold captials. If they are in another color, including white, you should be checking the authenticity. Or if they have mixed cases they could also be fake.
  • An item is most likely counterfeit if it has a generic label that reads something along the lines of "65% COTTON 35% POLYESTER". Plain symbols and MADE IN CHINA on the label will also prove to be inauthentic.
  • Every garment piece has their own label, and sometimes they differ from eachother. Some fakes have created labels with a scan code to check authenticity, and there hasn't been genuine LOUIS VUITTON products we have seen that carry this label.

How To Identify Real PRADA

  • Fake PRADA tags with have the wrong thickness. Make sure you are checking if the tag is thick in feeling, not thin.
  • Check and see what the color of the font text is. If the PRADA brand name is blue or gray, then you are most likley looking at a fake. White is the correct color the label text should have.
  • When you look at the code line for the garment, look it up yourself. That line should match the type of garment exactly, and if it does not it was stolen from a different garment.
